What it does

Real prey never runs in circles. Neither does this.

Sun on the floor, and nothing has moved since noon. Here is what changes.

  1. i

    It moves like prey.

    At your cat's pace, never in a loop. Every move is chosen live.

  2. ii

    Every hunt ends with a catch.

    Not a red dot that can never be caught. Your cat gets to finish what it starts.

  3. iii

    It plays on its own.

    It runs play sessions by itself and stops when your cat loses interest.

  4. Illustration
    iv

    Multi-layered AI.

    Several AI systems, working as one: they read your cat, choose the next move and shape it as it happens. Endless variations, no preset loops.

  5. Illustration
    v

    It learns your cat.

    It notices which moves your cat chases and which it ignores. Overnight, it trains on the day's play, so tomorrow it plays more of what your cat loves.

  6. vi

    You're in charge.

    You control it from your phone: start play, stop it, or let it run its own sessions.

Straight answers

What is it, exactly?

A small robot that moves a fishing-rod toy like prey. It plays on its own, learns how your cat likes to play, and you control it from your phone.

Can I buy one?

Not yet. The waitlist hears first when orders open.

Will my cat get bored of it?

It never plays a loop. It learns which moves your cat chases and which it walks away from, and keeps changing as your cat does.

Read the film's words

For a moment, you're a cat. Eyes low, ears up, whiskers forward.

Hour six. It's the sixth hour of an empty afternoon. The sun has crawled halfway across the floor, and nothing in this house has moved in hours. Your world sits a hand's width off the ground. You've been waiting all day for something, anything, to happen.

Something moved. Then, at the edge of the rug, a feather twitches. Your pupils swell until the whole room goes soft and only that one point stays sharp.

Freeze. It stops dead. So do you. Every muscle loads like a spring, because anything that holds this still is hiding something.

Dart. It streaks across the sunlight and vanishes behind the cushion. A long pause. Then the tip of it shows again, testing the air. Real prey never runs in circles. It runs, it hides, and it checks whether you're still watching. So does this.

Pounce. Now. Got it. Both paws, every claw, the whole long afternoon spent in a single leap. Every hunt deserves an ending you can catch.

Who's moving the toy? Meet the Prekko Pawbot™. It runs play sessions on its own and stops when your cat loses interest. It doesn't play back a loop. Every move is chosen live, for your cat. Working prototype shown; final design may differ.

Multi-layered AI. Several AI systems, working as one. See: It reads your cat in real time: where they are, how fast they move, where they look, how low they crouch and how interested they are. (Illustration, not the real model.) Choose: A strategy layer reads your cat's mood and picks the moves your cat chases. Move: A motion layer fine-tunes every move as it happens. Endless variations. No preset loops.

It learns your cat. Every session teaches it something: which moves make your cat crouch and chase, and which ones they walk away from. (Illustration of how it learns, not recorded data.) Overnight, it trains on the day's play and comes back sharper. It learns how your cat plays and gets better at playing with your cat over time.

Your cat's day. You control it from your phone. A planned tracker in the app would keep the story of each day: when the hunts happened, how long they lasted, and the quiet stretches in between. (Planned feature, not in the prototype shown; example numbers.)
