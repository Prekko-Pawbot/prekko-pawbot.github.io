Privacy

What we collect

When you join the waitlist we store your email address, the time you signed up, and the utm_* campaign tags from the link you arrived on (for example which post or ad sent you). Nothing else is asked for. The list is stored with Cloudflare, which runs the small service that receives the form.

Visits

We may count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics: pages viewed, the site that sent you, country and device type. It sets no cookies and does not identify you.

What it is used for

Your address is used only for launch updates about Prekko: a welcome email, occasional progress news, and the launch announcement. To deliver those emails it is sent to Resend, the service that sends them. We do not sell it.

Leaving the list

Every email has an unsubscribe link. One tap and you get nothing further from us.

Ads

This page may run a Meta pixel so ads can be measured. When enabled, the pixel records page visits and sign-ups, not your email address.

Questions: reply to any of our emails.
